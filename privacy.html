Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Meld is published by Meld Systems LLC. This policy covers what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. Meld has no accounts, no sign-in, and currently no online functionality. There is no server to send anything to.

Your files

Scenes, models, textures and imported images are stored on your device. We have no access to them and cannot recover them, so keep your own backups of anything that matters.

When you import a file, the system grants access to that file alone. Meld does not read anything you have not chosen.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the app store you bought through. Your payment details go to them and are never seen by us.

Diagnostics

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with developers on your device, we may receive aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. This is a platform feature and can be turned off in your device settings.

No tracking

Meld contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no third party trackers. Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

Support email

If you email us, we receive what you send and use it to answer you.

Children

Meld collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

Online features such as cloud saves are planned. If a feature involves collecting or transmitting anything, this policy will be updated before it ships and the date above will change.
