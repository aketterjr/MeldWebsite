Support

For bugs, crashes or anything not working the way it should, email support@meld3d.app. It helps enormously if you include:

  • Which iPad you are using, and which version of iPadOS
  • The Meld version and build number, from Settings → Other
  • What you were doing when it happened

Billing and subscriptions

Meld subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple, not through us. To change or cancel a subscription, open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Refunds are handled by Apple and requested at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds directly, because we never receive your payment details — Apple does. If something went wrong, email us anyway and we will help you sort it out.

Press and business

For press enquiries, review copies, or anything commercial, email support@meld3d.app with "Press" or "Business" in the subject line.

Creators and educators: we are happy to provide complimentary access. Get in touch and tell us a little about what you make.
