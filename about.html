Meld

About Meld

Meld is a polygon modeling and texture painting app built for iPad. It is made by Meld Systems LLC, based in Dallas, Texas.

What Meld is

Meld is polygon modeling software, which means you build things out of flat surfaces joined along their edges. It is the approach behind most of the 3D you already see, buildings, vehicles, furniture, props, and characters in games and film. You start from a simple shape and work it toward what you had in mind: pull a face outward to extend a wall, round an edge so it catches the light, add a new edge where you want more detail.

Working this way gives you precision. Every edge sits where you put it, and the tools for shaping the underlying structure such as adding loops, sliding edges, controlling how a surface flows, are all direct. That control is what makes a model good to hand off, whether it is headed for a game engine, an animation rig, or a final render.

Paint with Colour, Roughness, Metallic and Emissive straight onto a model with Apple Pencil using pen pressure. Light the scene with point, spot, directional and area lights, and see it come together with ray-traced shadows and reflections rendered on device.

Taking your work with you

Meld exports to OBJ, glTF, GLB and USD, and imports the same formats. Start something here and finish it anywhere, or bring in a model from elsewhere and keep going. Your files are yours, in formats every other tool already speaks.

Who makes it

Meld is built by a single developer. The mesh engine, the renderer, the paint pipeline and the file format are all written specifically for this app rather than assembled from an existing engine.
